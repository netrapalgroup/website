Why transaction handling is different

Most software can tolerate an occasional glitch. Financial software cannot. A duplicated charge, a missing ledger entry or an unreconciled settlement becomes a customer complaint, an audit finding or a loss.

Our background is in building and operating systems where every transaction must be accounted for. We bring those habits to your platform.

  • Idempotent by design. Safe retries without double-processing.
  • Traceable end to end. Every movement of money can be explained.
  • Reconciled continuously. Differences are surfaced early, not at month end.
  • Secure by default. Least privilege, encryption and minimal data retention.
  • Built to be operated. Observability and runbooks for the people on call.
